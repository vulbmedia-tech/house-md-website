Treatments/Hair & Scalp

PRP for Hair

Uses your own platelet-rich plasma to support hair growth.

Good for

  • Thinning hair
  • Early hair loss

What to expect

A small blood sample is processed to concentrate the platelets, which are then injected into the scalp. Usually done as a series of sessions.

Before your visit

  • Book a consultation first so your doctor can check this treatment suits you.
  • Tell us about any medicines, allergies or recent treatments.

After your treatment

  • Don't wash your hair on the day unless advised.
  • Avoid hair dye and harsh scalp products for a few days.
