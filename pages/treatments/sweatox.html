Treatments/Body

Sweatox

Botox for heavy sweating on the underarms, palms and feet.

Good for

  • Excess underarm sweating
  • Sweaty palms
  • Sweaty feet

What to expect

Small injections across the treated area reduce sweat production. The effect builds over the following days.

Before your visit

  • Book a consultation first so your doctor can check this treatment suits you.
  • Tell us about any medicines, allergies or recent treatments.

After your treatment

  • Avoid heavy exercise and hot showers on the day.
  • Skip deodorant on the day of treatment unless your doctor says otherwise.
