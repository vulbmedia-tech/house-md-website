CO2 Fractional Laser

Laser resurfacing for acne scars, rough texture and enlarged pores.

Good for

  • Acne scars
  • Uneven, bumpy texture
  • Enlarged pores
  • Mixed scars, as part of a combination plan

What to expect

The laser creates tiny treatment zones in the skin to trigger repair and new collagen. Numbing cream is applied first. Expect redness and some peeling for several days as the skin renews.

Many patients plan CO2 in the rainy season, when it's easier to stay out of the sun.

Before your visit

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning in the weeks before.
  • Tell your doctor about any medicines, skin products or history of cold sores.

After your treatment

  • Use the gentle cleanser and moisturiser your doctor recommends.
  • Wear sunscreen daily and stay out of direct sun.
  • Don't pick at peeling skin.
