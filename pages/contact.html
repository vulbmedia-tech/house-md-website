Ask a question
Do I need a consultation before a treatment?

Yes. Every treatment starts with an assessment by our doctors, so your plan matches your skin, your goals and your schedule.

Is there downtime?

It depends on the treatment. Cosmelan can cause redness and peeling in the first days. CO2 Fractional Laser needs some recovery time between sessions. Botox results settle over about two weeks. Your doctor will walk you through aftercare.

How many sessions will I need?

Some treatments are one in-clinic session plus home care, like Cosmelan. Others build over a series, like Diode Laser and Pro Contour, because results develop over time. Your plan depends on your skin.

How do I prepare for Diode Laser hair removal?

Shave a day before and avoid waxing, since the laser needs the hair root to target. Avoid sun exposure and tanning before and after your sessions.

Do you treat skin conditions, not just aesthetic concerns?

Yes. Dr. Aira Pampolina, MD, DPDS, sees patients for skin, hair and nail conditions such as psoriasis, warts (kulugo), alopecia and rashes that keep coming back.
