Preparation & aftercare

Before and after your treatment

General tips to help you get the most out of your visit. Your doctor's instructions for your treatment always come first.

Before your consultation

  • List the skincare and medicines you use
  • Share past treatments and reactions
  • Bring photos of what bothers you, if it helps

Before laser treatments

  • Avoid sun and tanning
  • For Diode Laser, shave a day before. Don't wax or pluck
  • Tell us about medicines that make skin sun-sensitive

After lasers & peels

  • Keep skincare gentle
  • Wear sunscreen every day
  • Let peeling skin shed on its own

After injectables

  • Don't rub or massage the area
  • Skip heavy exercise for the day
  • Give results about two weeks to settle
