Skin concerns/Common concern

Acne & Acne Scars

Active breakouts, the marks they leave and textured scars.

What it can look like

  • Recurring breakouts or clogged pores
  • Dark marks after pimples heal
  • Rolling, boxcar or ice-pick scars
  • Uneven, bumpy texture

Common causes

  • Excess oil and clogged pores
  • Hormonal changes
  • Inflammation that marks the skin as it heals
  • Picking or squeezing pimples

How we treat it

Active acne is calmed first, then scars are treated once the skin has settled. Many patients do best with a combination plan, such as CO2 Fractional Laser with SkinPen.
