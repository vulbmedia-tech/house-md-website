Excess Sweating

Heavy sweating on the underarms, palms or feet.

What it can look like

  • Sweat marks on clothes
  • Sweaty palms that get in the way of daily tasks
  • Sweaty feet

Common causes

  • Overactive sweat glands
  • Heat and humidity
  • Stress

How we treat it

Sweatox reduces sweat production in the treated area, so you can stop planning outfits around sweat marks.
