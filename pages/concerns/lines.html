Skin concerns/Common concern

Fine Lines & Wrinkles

Expression lines, early creases and skin that feels less firm.

What it can look like

  • Forehead and frown lines
  • Crow's feet when you smile
  • Lines that stay when your face is relaxed
  • Skin that feels less firm

Common causes

  • Repeated facial expressions
  • Natural collagen loss with age
  • Sun exposure
  • Dehydrated skin

How we treat it

Expression lines respond well to Botox, while skin quality and firmness improve with skinboosters and radiofrequency. The goal is to look refreshed, not frozen.
